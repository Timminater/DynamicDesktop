Common questions and troubleshooting

DynamicDesktop keeps flickering or restarting. How do I fix it?

If your regular Windows wallpaper changes automatically, each change can make DynamicDesktop flicker or restart. Use a fixed background that does not rotate.

  1. Open Windows Settings → Personalization → Background.
  2. Set ‘Personalize your background’ to ‘Picture’ or ‘Solid color’. Do not use ‘Slideshow’ or ‘Windows spotlight’.
  3. Restart DynamicDesktop after changing this setting.
Changes are saved automatically and applied to your desktop right away
